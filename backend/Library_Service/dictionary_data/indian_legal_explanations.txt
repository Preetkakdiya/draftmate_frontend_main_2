# Legal Glossary — "D" Terms

term: damage
explanation: Harm or injury to a person, property, or rights, whether physical, financial, or reputational.

term: damages
explanation: Monetary compensation awarded by a court to a party who has suffered loss or injury because of another's act or omission.

term: damages, actual
explanation: Compensation awarded for the real, provable loss suffered, as opposed to nominal or punitive amounts.

term: damages, consequential
explanation: Damages that do not flow directly from an act but result indirectly from its consequences, such as lost profits.

term: damages, double or treble
explanation: Damages multiplied by statute (typically two or three times actual damages) as a penalty for certain wrongful conduct.

term: damages, incidental
explanation: Reasonable costs incurred by an injured party in dealing with a breach, such as inspection, transportation, or storage expenses.

term: damages, liquidated
explanation: An amount the parties agree in advance will serve as compensation for a specific breach, set out in the contract itself.

term: damages, punitive
explanation: Damages awarded beyond actual loss to punish egregious or malicious conduct and deter similar future behavior.

term: damnum absque injuria
explanation: Latin for "loss without legal injury" — harm suffered for which the law provides no remedy because no legal right was violated.

term: damnum fatale
explanation: Loss caused by an unforeseeable natural event, akin to an act of God.

term: dangerous
explanation: Having the capacity to cause harm or injury.

term: dangerous instrumentality
explanation: An object or device inherently likely to cause serious injury, imposing heightened duties of care on those who control it.

term: dangerous weapon
explanation: Any object capable of causing death or serious bodily harm when used as a weapon.

term: day in court
explanation: A party's opportunity to be heard and to present their case before a tribunal.

term: dead hand
explanation: The control exercised by a deceased person over property or affairs through a will or trust, often referring to restrictions imposed long after death.

term: deadly force
explanation: Force used with the intent or likely effect of causing death or serious bodily harm.

term: deadly weapon
explanation: An instrument capable of inflicting death or grievous bodily injury.

term: deal
explanation: A transaction or agreement between parties.

term: dealer
explanation: A person or entity that buys and sells goods, securities, or property as a regular business.

term: death
explanation: The permanent cessation of all vital functions, ending legal personhood.

term: death benefit
explanation: A payment made to a beneficiary upon the death of an insured or plan participant, typically under a life insurance policy or pension.

term: death certificate
explanation: An official document issued by a government authority recording the fact, date, and cause of a person's death.

term: death penalty
explanation: Capital punishment; a sentence of execution imposed for the most serious crimes.

term: death records
explanation: Official governmental records documenting deaths, used for legal, statistical, and genealogical purposes.

term: death tax
explanation: A colloquial term for estate or inheritance tax levied on property transferred at death.

term: debauch
explanation: To corrupt or lead someone into immoral or dissolute behavior.

term: debauchery
explanation: Excessive indulgence in sensual or immoral pleasures.

explanation: An unsecured debt instrument issued by a corporation or government, backed only by the issuer's general creditworthiness.

term: debit
explanation: An entry recording an amount owed or a deduction from an account.

term: debt
explanation: A sum of money or obligation owed by one party to another.

term: debt, bonded
explanation: Debt secured by bonds issued by a corporation or government.

term: debt, floating
explanation: Short-term debt that is continually renewed or refinanced rather than paid off.

term: debt, secured
explanation: Debt backed by collateral that the creditor may seize if the debtor defaults.

term: debtor
explanation: A person or entity that owes money or an obligation to another.

term: debtor, judgment
explanation: A debtor against whom a court has entered a judgment for the amount owed.

term: decease
explanation: To die; the legal end of a person's life.

term: deceased
explanation: A person who has died.

term: decedent
explanation: A deceased person, especially referenced in probate or estate matters.

term: deceit
explanation: A false representation made knowingly, intending to induce another to act to their detriment.

term: deceive
explanation: To intentionally mislead someone through false statements or conduct.

term: decide
explanation: To reach a determination or judgment on a disputed matter.

term: decision
explanation: A court's or tribunal's formal resolution of the issues in a case.

term: declaration
explanation: A formal statement of fact, often made under oath or as part of a pleading.

term: declaration against interest
explanation: A statement made by a person, contrary to their own interest at the time, admissible as an exception to the hearsay rule.

term: Declaration of Independence
explanation: The 1776 document by which the American colonies declared their separation from Great Britain.

term: declaration of war
explanation: A formal announcement by a government that a state of war exists with another nation.

term: declaratory
explanation: Serving to state or clarify rights or legal relations without ordering specific relief.

explanation: A court ruling that establishes the rights and legal relations of the parties without awarding damages or ordering action.

term: declaratory statute
explanation: A statute enacted to clarify or remove doubt about the meaning of existing law.

term: declare
explanation: To formally state or announce.

term: decree
explanation: An official order or judgment issued by a court, especially in equity or family law matters.

term: decree nisi
explanation: A provisional court decree, such as in divorce proceedings, that becomes final unless cause is shown against it within a set time.

term: decriminalize
explanation: To remove criminal penalties from an act previously classified as a crime.

term: dedicate
explanation: To set apart land or property for public use.

explanation: To subtract an amount, especially for tax purposes.
